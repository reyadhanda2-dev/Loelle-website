Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy explains how information may be handled when you use Loelle, a personal AI assistant project.

Information You Provide

When you send a message to Loelle, your message may be processed to generate a response. Depending on the current configuration, messages may be sent to Google's Gemini API for processing.

Third-Party Services

Loelle may use third-party AI services to generate responses. These providers process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. Please review the relevant provider policies.

Storage and Retention

Whether messages are stored, and for how long, depends on Loelle's actual implementation and configuration. Avoid submitting passwords, financial details, or other highly sensitive information.

Information Security

Reasonable precautions should be taken to protect information handled by Loelle. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Loelle is a personal AI assistant project. Its owner should ensure that its use and any information processing meet applicable age-related requirements.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as Loelle's features and data handling change. The date above should be updated whenever this policy changes.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the project owner using a contact method published by the owner.
